★ Know the signs

Six ways roofs fail

Hail damage

Bruised shingles are only part of it — collateral hits to vents, flashing, and gutters are what a trained inspection catches and a drive-by misses.

Wind damage

Careful: patching wind damage the wrong way in Indiana can void your coverage and cost you a full replacement you were owed.

Dry rot

Dry-rotted rubber pipe boots are one of the most common leak points — small part, big water problem once the barrier is breached.

Mechanical damage

Uncertified installers can void warranties and take years off a brand-new roof. When it has to be done right, call people who do it every day.

Blistering

Poor ventilation cooks shingles from both sides until they blister — and blisters turn into leaks faster than most owners expect.

Worn-out roof

Crumbling, curling, or missing shingles can void insurance on the whole house. If your roof looks beat up, it's time for an honest look.
