★ The process

What happens when you call.

1

We look

A free inspection of the roof and full exterior, with photo documentation of everything we find. If nothing's wrong, we tell you that too.

2

You get a straight answer

A sit-down with Jalen or Joseph: what we found, what it means, and your options — without pressure or obligation.

3

We advocate

If you file a claim, we meet your adjuster on site with weather reports, EagleView aerial measurements, estimates, and supplements.

4

We build

You pick your colors, we do the work — no money down unless otherwise agreed, balance due at completion, and a 5-year labor warranty behind it.

Every policy is different. We document damage and work directly with your carrier, but coverage decisions always rest with your insurer and the terms of your policy.
